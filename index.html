❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why are my textures missing?

Missing textures are often caused by incorrect file paths or unsupported formats.

What makes a model too heavy?

High polygon count, large textures, and complex shaders can affect performance.

How can I fix slow rendering?

Optimize geometry, reduce texture sizes, and simplify shaders.

Can I automate debugging?

Yes. Tools can automatically detect common issues in assets and pipelines.
